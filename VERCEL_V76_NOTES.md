# V76 — Product Image Layer Cleanup

- Removed decorative/shine layers from product cards.
- Product card image area uses a plain background.
- Product images use `mix-blend-mode:multiply` so white/cream image backgrounds do not form a visible capsule/layer over the card.
- Image remains centered with `object-fit:contain`.
- Save button and badge remain normal layers above the product image.
- No product facts or affiliate URLs were invented or changed.
